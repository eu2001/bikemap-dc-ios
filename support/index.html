About BikeMap DC

BikeMap DC is a community-curated cycling map for the Washington, D.C. metro area. It shows every protected bike lane, conventional bike lane, bus/bike lane, contraflow lane, signed route, off-street trail, mountain bike trail, and unpaved trail across DC, Arlington, Alexandria, Falls Church, Tysons, Bethesda, Silver Spring, College Park, and Prince George's County. It also pins thousands of Capital Bikeshare stations, fix-it stands, bike shops, water fountains, Metro stations, and commuter-rail stops — plus a layer of cyclist accident locations from the past four years of DC Vision Zero data.

Users in the DC area can contribute new points and report bike thefts or cyclist accidents. All submissions are reviewed by a moderator before appearing on the map.

Quick how-to

I can't sign up

  • Use an email address you control — confirmation is automatic.
  • Pick a username that isn't taken. Usernames are case-insensitive.
  • Passwords must be at least 6 characters.

The map looks empty

  • Check the Layers panel (hamburger icon, top-left). All cycle-route categories except Unpaved Trail and Mountain Bike Trail are on by default.
  • If POIs (Bike Parking, Capital Bikeshare, etc.) don't load, kill and reopen the app — the first sync needs a stable connection.

Adding a point or report

  • You can only add new points or file theft / accident reports while you are physically within 5 miles of downtown DC.
  • Tap the map exactly where the point is, drag the pin to fine-tune, then fill in the title and submit.
  • All submissions are reviewed by a moderator and usually appear on the map within 24 hours.

I want to delete my account

  • Tap your avatar (top-right of the map) → Edit Profile (blue pencil) → "Delete account" in the Danger zone.
  • Your profile, registered bikes, and photos are deleted permanently. Your map contributions remain anonymously on the map so they stay useful to the community.

Privacy

We collect the minimum needed to run a community map: your email and chosen username for sign-in, your registered bikes (so you have their info handy if stolen), and your approximate location while the app is in the foreground (so points can be pinned where you are). We never sell data and never run ads.

Full privacy policy: https://sites.google.com/view/bikemapdc/privacy

Report a problem with the map

Found an outdated bike lane, a missing fix-it stand, or a wrong location? Email us with the point's reference code (e.g. BP4231) — every point in the app shows its code in the title for easy reference.
