Welcome to BikeMap DC

BikeMap DC is a community-powered cycling map for the Washington, D.C. metro area. It pulls together everything a cyclist needs to plan a trip, find a parking rack, fix a flat, or report a problem — all in one place, on your phone.

What you'll find on the map:

  • 5,500+ bike-infrastructure segments — protected lanes, bike lanes, contraflow lanes, bus/bike lanes, shared lanes, signed routes, off-street trails, unpaved trails, and mountain-bike trails.
  • 5,700+ points of interest — bike parking, Capital Bikeshare stations, fix-it stands, bike shops, water fountains, public restrooms, Metro stations, commuter-rail stops, recreation centers, and landmarks.
  • 1,700+ cyclist crash locations from the past 4 years (MPD Vision Zero).
  • Your fellow cyclists' contributions — bike thefts, new fix-it stands, recent accidents.

Coverage area: DC + Bethesda · Silver Spring · Tysons · McLean · Arlington · Falls Church · Alexandria · Capitol Heights · College Park · Hyattsville · New Carrollton · Suitland.

Getting started

Create your account

  1. Open the app.
  2. Tap Create account.
  3. Pick a username and a password (at least 6 characters).
  4. Choose one of our 10 DC-fauna avatars — bobcat, cardinal, crow, eagle, fox, otter, owl, raccoon, skunk, or squirrel.
  5. Tap Create account. You're in.

Sign in

Use the email and password you registered with. Tap the eye icon to reveal your password if you need to check it.

Forgot your password? Tap Forgot my password and we'll email a reset link.

Reading the map

The 9 cycle-route line styles

Open the legend at any time by tapping the hamburger icon (top-left). Each route type has a distinct color and dash style:

Protected Bike Lane — separated by a curb or barrier.
Bike Lane — painted dedicated lane on the road.
Contraflow Lane — bikes go against the one-way flow.
Bus/Bike Lane — shared with transit buses.
Shared Lane (Sharrow) — pavement arrows; cyclist may take the lane.
On-Street Signed Route — marked with signs only.
Off-Street Trail — paved multi-use path.
Unpaved Trail — gravel or natural-surface paths.
Mountain Bike Trail — single-track / MTB routes.

Tapping a point

Tap any marker → a sheet slides up with the category, full title, description, and the contributor's username. Every imported point has a reference code (e.g. BP4231) so you can quote it when reporting a problem.

Layers — show or hide categories

Tap the hamburger icon (top-left) to open the Bike Map legend.

  • Each row is a toggle. Tap to show / hide that category.
  • Show all — turn every category on.
  • Clear map — turn every category off.

By default, rare/loud layers (Unpaved Trail, Mountain Bike Trail) and the report layers (Theft, Accidents) are off — the map looks clean on first launch.

Map gestures

  • Pan: drag with one finger.
  • Zoom: pinch, or double-tap to zoom in, two-finger-tap to zoom out.
  • Recenter on yourself: tap the navigation arrow (bottom-right).
  • Zoom controls: + and − buttons (bottom-right).

Finding stuff fast

Tip: isolate one layer for a single-purpose view — turn off everything except 🚲 Bike Parking and the map becomes a parking-only finder. Same trick works for any category.
  • Need a bike rack? Toggle off everything except 🚲 Bike Parking.
  • Headed to a Metro? Turn on 🅼 Metro and zoom to your destination.
  • Bottle empty? Turn on 💧 Water Fill Stations.
  • Need a fix? Turn on 🔧 Fix-it Stand — most are 24/7 with a pump + tools.

Contributing to the map

You can add new points or file reports as long as you're physically within 5 miles of downtown DC (we check by GPS to keep submissions honest).

Add a new point

  1. Open the legend (hamburger icon).
  2. Tap the blue Add point button at the bottom.
  3. Pick a category.
  4. The blue banner "Tap the map to add a point" appears.
  5. Tap roughly where the point is, then drag the pin to fine-tune.
  6. Fill in a Title (required) and an optional Description.
  7. Tap Add [category].

A moderator reviews every submission before it appears publicly.

Report a bike theft

  1. Open the legend → tap the red Report Theft button.
  2. Read the safety reminder and tap Confirm.
  3. Tap the location on the map.
  4. Fill in: incident date/time, description, optional photo, and contact info (required) so the community can reach you.
  5. Tap Report Theft.
  6. If it was less than 24 h ago, choose "Yes, alert" to push-notify nearby BikeMap users.
Important: BikeMap is community help, not a police report. Always file a police report with MPD too.

Report a cyclist accident

  1. Open the legend → tap the orange Report Accident button.
  2. "If anyone is injured or in danger, call 911 immediately" — tap Continue.
  3. Tap the location, drag the pin, fill in Title + Description.
  4. Tap Add Cyclist Accidents.

Managing your account

My Profile

Tap your avatar in the top-right corner of the map. You'll see:

  • Avatar, username, edit pencil
  • Administrator Panel (admins only — see below)
  • My bikes — with photos, brand, color, etc.
  • Contributions — every approved point you've submitted, with its reference code
  • Total points (drives the ranking)
  • Sign out

Register a bike

  1. My Profile → Register your bike.
  2. Tap the photo placeholder to attach an image from your gallery.
  3. Fill in nickname, type (Conventional or E-bike), brand, color, wheel size, serial number, and identifying details.
  4. Tap Register bike.

Why register? If your bike is stolen, you'll have every detail ready for the police report, the manufacturer, and the community alert.

Edit your profile

My Profile → blue pencil. You can change:

  • Avatar (5×2 grid — tap to select)
  • Language — System, English, or Español
  • Change password
  • Delete account (Danger zone)

Delete your account

Edit Profile → Danger zone → Delete account → confirm. Your profile, bikes, and photos are deleted permanently. Your map contributions stay on the map anonymously.

Ranking

Tap the trophy icon (top-right of the map). 🥇 🥈 🥉 for the top 3 contributors. Your row is highlighted with "(you)".

Notifications

If you grant notification permission, you'll receive:

  • Bike theft alerts when a new theft is reported within ~24 h near your location.
  • Moderation alerts (admins only) when new submissions need review — plus a red dot on your avatar.

Manage notifications anytime in your phone's Settings → Apps → BikeMap DC → Notifications.

For moderators

If your account is flagged as an admin, you'll see a red dot on your avatar (top-right of the map) when there are pending submissions, and an Administrator Panel tile right under your profile card with a count.

Reviewing a submission

Tap Administrator Panel → each pending point has a mini-map, description, author, and a 3-button row:

  • Edit before approving (blue) — fix title, description, or pin location.
  • Reject (gray/red) — discards the submission (doesn't count against the author's ranking).
  • Approve (green) — publishes to the map (increments the author's contribution count).

Editing or deleting a published point

Tap any pin on the map → the Map Point sheet → Edit icon (top-left of the sheet), or Delete point in the Danger zone at the bottom.

Tips & tricks

  • Long tap a marker to keep the sheet open while you look up the address.
  • Want a clean route map? Turn off all POI categories — just cycle routes remain.
  • Pinch and zoom into a dense area (e.g. Logan Circle) to see individual bike parking racks.
  • If GPS is acting up, the navigation arrow re-centers you at the Washington Monument as a fallback.
  • If you're more than 10 miles from DC, the app opens at the Washington Monument instead of your location.
  • Every imported point has a reference code in its title. Spot an outdated one? Email bikemap.dc@gmail.com with the code.
